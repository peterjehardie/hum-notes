Orchestral samples in this folder come from VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (VSCO 2 CE)
by Versilian Studios: recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell, sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote.
Upright piano sampled by Simon Dalzell (Ivy Audio).
